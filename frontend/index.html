Donor locations stay private

Matching uses each donor's distance, but no patient, doctor or admin ever sees where a donor is. Donors switch themselves to inactive at any time.

Wellbeing support with a private journal and a chatbot

Patients rate each day from 1 to 5 and choose which entries a doctor may read. When the chatbot reads signs of serious distress, it points to a doctor or a helpline.

Which 4 jobs does the AI do?

Report summaries, donor ranking, mood trends and the chatbot. It never decides on treatment, and the rest keeps working when it is down.

Who can read a patient's medical history?

The patient and the treating doctor. Journal entries go only to the doctors the patient picks.

How do patients pay a hospital bill?

In cash at the hospital, by card through SSLCommerz, or by bKash.

What do the hospital referral statuses mean?

Pending, Accepted, Declined or More information needed. A declined referral comes with other hospitals to try.

Is the software certified for clinical use?

No. It is an academic project, not a certified medical system.
